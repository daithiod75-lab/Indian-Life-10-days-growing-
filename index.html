Program Framework & Objectives

Welcome to Your 10-Day India Experiential Journey

A 10-day trip to India is an intense, transformative, and high-tempo experience. This web application is designed to help you capture your daily observations, manage the rapid pace of travel, and synthesize your journey into a comprehensive final report.

📓 Part 1: Daily Field Diary (Days 1–10)

Each day features specific guiding prompts and focus areas (e.g., sensory landscapes, cultural interactions, historical sites, and personal resilience). Record your immediate reflections daily before memories fade!

📑 Part 2: The Final Report

Transform your daily diary entries into an analytical academic document. Follow the structured thematic sections: Executive Summary, Thematic Reflections, Chronological Highlights, Challenges & Resilience, and Conclusions.

💾

Automatic Local Save: All your progress is automatically saved to your browser's local storage as you type. You can safely close the browser and return anytime without losing data.
